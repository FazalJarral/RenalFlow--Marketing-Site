From patient intake to monthly review

A dialysis workflow, not a blank database.

RenalFlow follows the path of care your center already runs every week.

01

Add patient

Capture internal code, demographics, dialysis start date, diagnosis, access type, allergies, and medications.

02

Schedule session

Create regular days, shift, next session time, and machine number. Scheduled sessions appear on the dashboard.

03

Record vitals

Technicians track pre, during, and post-session vitals while the session moves from scheduled to completed.

04

Update labs

Technicians enter panel-based matrix labs while doctors compare recent dates and abnormal values.

05

Create diet plan

Nutritionists set renal targets, meals, avoid/limit lists, and notes informed by patient labs.

06

Review progress

Export patient summaries and diet plans, review trends, and keep every update center-scoped.
